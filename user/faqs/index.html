Your account

Questions and answers

Answers about finding providers, contact details and your account.

How do I find a provider?
Search by service and area, or browse a category. Results show providers who serve the area you chose.
What do the checks on a profile mean?
Each profile shows which details were checked — such as the mobile number and the documents an admin reviewed — and the date of each check.
How do I contact a provider?
Open their profile and choose Show contact. While you are logged in you can see contact details for a limited number of providers a day.
A category says “Coming soon”. What can I do?
Send an enquiry from that page. The team reads every enquiry and tries to match you with a provider by hand.
Can I list a business with this account?
Your role is chosen once, when you register. To list a business, register a separate account with your business mobile number and choose Provider.
Why are my mobile number and email partly hidden?
Summaries show contact details masked so they are not exposed on a shared screen.
How do I log out?
Use Log out at the top of the page or on your profile card. Always log out on a shared device.
What is stored about me in this demo?
Your name, mobile number, email address and chosen role, in a demo file on this server. There are no passwords, and the codes are fixed demo values.

Still need help?

Send an enquiry and the team will get back to you.
